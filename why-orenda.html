Why Orenda

Before I acquired anything, I had already built something.

Trust is essential. Trust alone is not enough. A real succession also requires certainty. So I built that certainty before I ever knocked on a founder's door.

I studied the search fund model, structured Orenda Continuum Capital around it, and earned the commitment of thirteen experienced investors who share one conviction:

That the right company, led the right way, is worth protecting for the long term.

Unlike most acquisitions, a traditional search fund begins long before a company is identified. These investors chose to support my search first. They spent months getting to know my experience, my values, my leadership style, and my vision for preserving and growing one exceptional business.
