Network Participation Agreement

Welcome to Rym’s Network

A handshake, put into words.

I’m personally searching for one exceptional Canadian business to acquire and lead — not on behalf of a firm, but as its next owner. Rym’s Network exists for one reason: to help me meet business owners who may be starting to think about retirement or succession. If that sounds like something you’d like to be part of, this page explains how it works.

The Mission

Welcome

Every great Canadian business deserves a thoughtful next chapter. I started this network to meet owners who may be considering succession, so I can find the one business I’m meant to carry forward. You’re part of that search — simply by being someone I trust.

The Simple Version

How the Network Works

1

You know a business owner who may be thinking about succession.

2

You introduce us.

3

If I acquire that business, I pay you a success fee on closing day.

Fair Play

First Introduction Rule

If more than one person introduces me to the same owner, the success fee belongs to whoever introduced them first. I keep a simple record of email introductions, and that record determines who was first.

Good To Know

What Counts as an Introduction

A introduction means the owner has agreed to be introduced and we both receive that introduction directly; typically by email. Mentioning my name in passing doesn’t count; I need to actually be connected.

Trust, Both Ways

Confidentiality

I’ll keep your personal information confidential. In turn, I ask that you respect the privacy of any business owner you introduce me to. Succession is a sensitive, personal decision for them.

No Pressure

No Obligation

Joining is free, and nobody owes anybody anything. You’re never required to make an introduction, I’m never required to pursue any particular acquisition, and no business owner is ever obligated to selltalk to me.

The Reward

Success Fee

If your introduction directly leads to a completed acquisition, I’ll pay you the agreed fixed success fee on the day the deal closes. The principle is: good introductions are rewarded.

Thank You

One Last Thing

Thank you for being here. You never know which conversation leads to the right introduction. And for one entrepreneur, that introduction could be the start of finding the right successor. I’m grateful to have you in my corner.
