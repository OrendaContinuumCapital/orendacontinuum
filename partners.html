For M&A advisors and referral partners

I am an investor-operator looking to acquire one established business that I will personally lead and grow for the long term.

I work with M&A advisors, business brokers, accountants, lawyers, bankers, wealth advisors and other professionals who advise business owners considering succession or a sale.

Acquisition capital is ready, and I am actively reviewing opportunities across Canada and the United States.

Acquisition criteria

EBITDA: C$2 million to C$8 million

Business type: B2B services and manufacturing

Geography: Canada and the United States

What I value: Established businesses with strong customer relationships, healthy margins, capable teams, and opportunities for long-term growth.

Not pursuing: Construction, technology, or real estate.

Why send an opportunity to me?

I am not building a portfolio of companies. I am looking for one business that I can personally operate.

My background is in engineering, operations and business leadership. I have led manufacturing operations across Canada and understand the responsibility that comes with succeeding an owner who may have spent decades building a company.

For the right opportunity, you are introducing the owner directly to the person who intends to lead the business after the transaction.
